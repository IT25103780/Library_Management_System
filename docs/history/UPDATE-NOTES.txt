UPDATE: Automatic book references and grouped card numbers

New books receive a fixed LUM reference automatically. Existing references are preserved.
The next number starts above the largest existing LUM reference and the saved counter.
The form preview is provisional; the final reference is allocated when saving.
Concurrent saves are protected by a database lock and transaction retry.
Deleting a book does not lower the counter. Failed saves roll back the allocation.
Numbers use at least four digits and continue beyond LUM-9999.
Existing databases are upgraded automatically on application startup.

The demo checkout displays 16 card digits as 1234 5678 9012 3456.
Typing, paste, validation and deletion around spaces are supported.
Demo card details remain browser-only. Live PayHere card entry stays on PayHere.
